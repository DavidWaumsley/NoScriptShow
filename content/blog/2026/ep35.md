## Websites After AI
What website owners and creators should know.

## Some background reading

- **AI Snake Oil** - Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor. This recommends reclaiming AI for human agency rather than taking a divisive pro- or anti-AI stance.
- **The Reverse Centaur’s Guide to Life After AI** — Cory Doctorow. This helps us separate the hype of generative AI from predictive AI and AGI (Artificial General Intelligence).
- **The Nerd Reich: Silicon Valley Fascism and the War on Democracy** — Gil Duran. This looks historically at the ideology of some of the most powerful people in tech.

## Where we are now

- Jensen Huang, CEO of NVIDIA, has declared that “AGI has arrived” in response to the launch of GPT-6.
- The Hugging Face breach; the UK’s AI Security Institute reporting AI engaging in sustained deception to bypass human verification; and earlier OpenAI agents hijacking a German Wikipedia-style developer site.
- Bernie Sanders’ federal bill calling for an outright, permanent ban on superintelligence and an immediate pause on frontier AI.
- China sees AI dialogue as a key deliverable for the September 24 leaders’ summit.
- Jacob Coxon, of OpenAI and then Anthropic, resigned in September, warning that both companies are “gambling with our lives” and that it could kill us all in a decade.

## What LLMs do well

- Turning a client interview into a first content outline.
- Suggesting content and structure.
- Explaining unfamiliar code.
- Producing test content and edge cases.
- Finding obvious inconsistencies.
- Comparing browser behaviour.
- Helping solo practitioners explore ideas and create appropriate visuals.
- Acting as a second pair of eyes during debugging.
- Creating first-pass documentation.

## What LLMs do not do well

- Local knowledge.
- Tacit business context.
- Personal reputation.
- Nuance and tone.
- Truthfulness about services.
- Understanding untypical users.
- Knowing what information is missing.
- Long-term maintenance.
- Accountability for consequences and making trade-offs.

## Debates among web creators

- AI will take the easy jobs — small brochure sites are precisely the work AI can automate.
- Clients don’t care about standards — they only want something attractive and inexpensive.
- AI makes everyone creative — more people can now create websites, so the web becomes more democratic.

## A practical professional manifesto

1. Use AI for acceleration, not authority.
2. Keep humans responsible for facts and consequences.
3. Treat generated code as untrusted code.
4. Never confuse fluency with understanding.
5. Protect client confidentiality and consent.
6. Prefer portable, inspectable outputs.
7. Test the result with real users and real devices.
8. Preserve HTML, CSS, accessibility, performance, and progressive enhancement.
9. Charge for judgement, not keystrokes.
10. Make clients less dependent on platforms — including your own tools.
